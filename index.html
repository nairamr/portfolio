Making integration knowledge accessible with AI.

I helped launch an internal AI-powered knowledge platform that gave sales, implementation and customer success teams easier access to technical and product information. It helped them prepare demos, answer onboarding questions and support customers through implementation.

See my other work

The problem

Customer onboarding involved sales, product, implementation and customer success. Each team needed reliable information about platform capabilities, available integrations, technical requirements and onboarding steps. That knowledge was scattered across documents and subject-matter experts, so even routine questions could require another conversation with engineering.

The solution

We launched an internal AI-assisted knowledge platform that made this information easier to find and understand. Sales teams used it to prepare customer conversations, implementation managers referenced technical requirements and onboarding guidance, and customer success, product teams and leadership could draw on the same information. It also supported API demonstrations using Postman.

My approach

I used AI-assisted prototyping to turn business requirements into interactive concepts and working demonstrations before engineering development began. These prototypes helped stakeholders visualize how integrations and onboarding workflows would work, including the customer experience, internal team responsibilities and key implementation steps. By reviewing working concepts early, we could identify gaps, refine requirements and align teams before committing engineering resources. Once development was complete, I supported end-to-end testing to validate workflows, identify issues and ensure the final implementation met business and customer expectations.

Outcome: A live internal knowledge platform used by sales, implementation, customer success, product and leadership, with API and Postman demos supporting customer conversations.
